# Veeza AI - 30s wedding / honeymoon comedy

_The brief exactly as supplied, used as the prompt for the generated video._

---

Create a 30-second cinematic comedy commercial about a young couple going through the chaos of planning their wedding while completely forgetting to plan their honeymoon.
The tone is funny, slightly brainrot, chaotic, romantic, and relatable, but still polished enough to feel like a real travel brand advertisement. Use realistic actors, cinematic lighting, expressive facial reactions, fast cuts, and comedic timing.
SCENE 1 — WEDDING PLANNING CHAOS | 0–6 seconds
Open with the couple in the middle of intense wedding planning.
Fast cuts:

* Bride surrounded by invitations, flowers, seating charts, and wedding decorations.
* Groom frantically talking on the phone with a vendor.
* A wedding cake nearly falls over.
* Someone walks past carrying way too many flowers.
* Bride gets a notification that something went wrong with the venue.
* Groom drops a stack of seating cards everywhere.

The bride looks exhausted.
Bride:
“Okay… flowers, venue, cake, DJ, seating chart…”
The groom suddenly looks up.
Groom:
“Wait. What about the honeymoon?”
Both freeze.
SCENE 2 — THE REALIZATION | 6–11 seconds
Close-up of the bride.
She slowly looks at her completely empty honeymoon planning page.
It says:
HONEYMOON
Nothing planned.
She looks at the groom.
Bride:
“…We forgot the honeymoon.”
Dramatic pause.
The groom looks genuinely horrified.
Groom:
“We're getting married in like… two weeks.”
Quick chaotic montage of them opening travel websites and getting overwhelmed by options, planning details, and travel requirements.
The groom closes the laptop.
Groom:
“I can't do another spreadsheet.”
SCENE 3 — “WAIT… VEEZA.” | 11–18 seconds
The bride suddenly grabs her phone.
Bride:
“Wait. There's Veeza.”
She opens Veeza, the AI travel agent.
The mood immediately shifts from chaotic to calm.
Show Veeza helping them organize their honeymoon and travel process:

* They tell Veeza where they want to go.
* Veeza helps organize the trip.
* Veeza identifies travel/visa requirements if applicable.
* Veeza helps prepare applications.
* Veeza verifies their documents.
* Veeza helps secure appointments when needed.
* Everything becomes organized in one simple process.

The groom watches.
Groom:
“Wait… that's actually it?”
The bride smiles.
Bride:
“That's it.”
SCENE 4 — THE WEDDING | 18–24 seconds
Transition into the actual wedding.
Beautiful cinematic shots:

* Bride walking down the aisle.
* Groom seeing her for the first time.
* They exchange vows.
* They laugh together.
* Guests applaud.
* They kiss.

The chaos is finally gone.
Then show them leaving the reception together.
The groom whispers:
Groom:
“So… we actually have a honeymoon?”
The bride smiles.
Bride:
“We actually have a honeymoon.”
They laugh.
SCENE 5 — THE RELIEF / PAYOFF | 24–30 seconds
Show the couple at the airport or arriving at their honeymoon destination.
They sit together, relaxed and happy, looking out at a beautiful tropical destination.
The groom exhales dramatically.
Groom:
“Finally.”
The bride leans against him.
Bride:
“Worth it.”
Show them walking together toward the beach / destination.
End card:
“Your trip shouldn’t die in paperwork.”
“Meet Veeza — your AI travel agent.”
Small supporting text:
Plan. Prepare. Travel.
End with the Veeza logo.
VISUAL STYLE
The first half should feel fast, chaotic, and slightly ridiculous, with quick zooms, frantic movements, notifications, papers everywhere, and exaggerated reactions.
When Veeza appears, the pacing becomes noticeably calmer and more organized.
The wedding scene should feel beautiful and emotional, creating a contrast with the earlier chaos.
The final honeymoon scene should feel like a huge sigh of relief — the couple finally gets to enjoy each other after surviving the wedding-planning madness.
The core message is:
Wedding planning is already chaotic. Don't let planning your trip become another headache. Veeza makes the travel process easier so you can focus on getting married and enjoying your honeymoon.
