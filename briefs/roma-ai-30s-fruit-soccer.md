Overall Style
Create a 30-second comedic AI advertisement for Roma AI with a surreal, funny “brainrot” aesthetic. The characters are cute anthropomorphic fruits, but the world around them looks like a realistic college campus and soccer field. The humor should come from treating the fruit characters' college and soccer lives completely seriously.
Visual style: polished cinematic 3D animation, highly expressive anthropomorphic fruit characters, realistic lighting and environments, exaggerated facial expressions, comedic timing, energetic camera movement, slightly absurd internet/brainrot humor.
Important: Keep the main character consistent throughout the entire video: a small anthropomorphic strawberry girl with a cute expressive face, tiny arms and legs, a college backpack, and a soccer uniform. She should remain recognizable in every scene.
