HIGGSFIELD VIDEO PROMPT — SCENE 1: THE BAR
Duration: 4 seconds
Style: Photorealistic, premium cinematic lifestyle commercial
Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
Scene purpose: Establish the protagonist's current lifestyle, create an upscale social atmosphere, and introduce a subtle feeling of reflection before the next character approaches.

ENVIRONMENT AND SET DESIGN
A sophisticated, upscale cocktail bar in a major American city at night. The bar feels exclusive and contemporary, with dark walnut wood, a polished black marble countertop, brushed brass accents, leather barstools, elegant pendant lights, and large floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking a softly illuminated urban skyline. Warm golden reflections shimmer across the glassware and polished surfaces. Small candle-like table lights create pockets of warm illumination throughout the room.
The establishment is comfortably busy on a typical evening. Approximately 12–18 adult patrons occupy the background, creating the atmosphere of a popular but refined cocktail lounge. The room is neither empty nor overcrowded. Every part of the background should feel naturally inhabited, with people behaving independently and realistically.
The color palette consists of rich charcoal, black, walnut brown, muted gold, warm amber, and subtle cream highlights. The overall aesthetic resembles a high-budget fashion or luxury lifestyle commercial shot on a professional cinema camera.

PRIMARY CHARACTER — FOREGROUND
A physically fit 28-year-old man with light skin, short tousled light-brown hair, a defined angular jawline, straight nose, clean-shaven face, and naturally athletic build. His facial features are realistic, proportionate, and consistent with the supplied character reference image.
He wears a tailored black two-piece suit, a crisp white dress shirt, and a neatly tied black necktie. The suit is fitted across his shoulders and chest without appearing excessively tight. His shirt collar is clean and structured, his lapels are sharply defined, and the fabric has a realistic matte finish. His appearance is polished and sophisticated, like a young professional after work.
He sits alone on a black leather barstool at the counter, positioned slightly to the left of the center of the vertical frame. His torso faces the bar at a slight angle, with one forearm resting naturally on the countertop. His other hand holds a short, heavy-bottomed crystal tumbler containing a small amount of amber-colored liquor and several partially melted ice cubes.
His posture is relaxed but subtly withdrawn. His shoulders are slightly lowered, and his expression is neutral and thoughtful. He does not look visibly distressed, drunk, or overtly unhappy. He appears to be quietly passing time in a familiar environment.

BACKGROUND PATRONS — NATURAL, CONTINUOUS ACTIVITY
The bar must feel genuinely alive. Background patrons remain visible throughout the entire four-second shot, moving subtly and independently rather than appearing like static extras.

* At the bar, behind the protagonist: Two well-dressed men in their late twenties sit side by side, quietly discussing something over their drinks. One leans slightly toward the other while speaking; the other responds with a small smile and a natural nod. Their mouths move realistically, but their conversation is not intelligible.
* At a nearby high-top table: A man and woman in their early thirties sit facing each other with cocktail glasses on the table. The woman gestures gently while speaking, and the man listens before giving a relaxed smile. Their movements are casual and unsynchronized.
* Near the far end of the counter: A bartender in a crisp dark shirt and black apron carefully pours a drink into a cocktail glass, places it on a small napkin, and turns to acknowledge another customer. The movements are fluid and physically accurate.
* In the middle distance: Three adult friends stand in a loose semicircle, chatting quietly. One briefly raises a glass while making a point, another laughs softly, and the third shifts their weight naturally.
* Near the windows: Additional patrons sit in small groups, their silhouettes and facial movements softly visible against the distant city lights.

All patrons wear believable evening attire: dark jackets, fitted shirts, elegant dresses, neutral blouses, and understated accessories. They should look like real adults enjoying an evening out, not posed fashion models.
Background movement must be subtle and varied. No two people should perform the same action at the same time. Some patrons lift glasses, others listen, turn their heads, gesture, or shift in their seats. No one looks into the camera. Nobody notices or interacts with the protagonist in this opening shot.

CAMERA DIRECTION AND SHOT COMPOSITION
Single continuous shot — no cuts.
Use a cinematic 35mm lens aesthetic with realistic perspective, shallow depth of field, and natural optical falloff. The image should feel captured by a professional cinematographer rather than generated or artificially sharpened.
0.0–1.0 seconds — Establish the atmosphere
Begin with a medium-wide view from a slightly oblique angle along the bar counter. The protagonist occupies the foreground, clearly identifiable in his black suit and white shirt. His face and upper body are the main focal point, while the surrounding patrons, bartender, and illuminated bar interior provide context behind him.
The polished counter extends diagonally into the background, creating depth and leading lines. Warm pendant lights and reflections form soft circular bokeh behind the protagonist. The camera begins a very slow, stable dolly-in toward him.
1.0–2.5 seconds — The drinking moment
Continue the gradual dolly-in. The protagonist lifts the crystal tumbler toward his mouth, takes one small, natural sip, and lowers the glass. His fingers wrap realistically around the tumbler, with subtle highlights refracting through the glass and amber liquid.
The ice cubes shift and lightly touch the sides as the glass moves. He places the tumbler gently on the marble countertop, producing a subtle, realistic glass-on-stone contact sound.
Behind him, the bartender continues preparing drinks, the seated patrons continue their conversations, and the groups in the middle distance make small, natural gestures. Their activity remains visible but slightly defocused.
2.5–4.0 seconds — Hold on the protagonist
The camera settles into a medium close-up, framing the protagonist from approximately mid-chest upward while keeping the glass visible near the lower edge of the frame.
He briefly looks down toward his drink, then shifts his gaze toward the room without turning fully toward the camera. His expression remains calm and contemplative, with a natural blink and subtle breathing.
The ambient background continues uninterrupted. The final frame should leave visual room on the right side of the composition for another person to approach in the following scene.
Do not introduce the second main character yet. This shot belongs entirely to the protagonist and the atmosphere surrounding him.

LIGHTING AND CINEMATOGRAPHY
Use warm practical lighting motivated by the actual pendant fixtures and bar lamps. A soft amber key light illuminates one side of the protagonist's face, while a restrained, cooler fill from the windows preserves detail in the shadows. A subtle warm rim light separates his dark suit from the background.
Maintain realistic skin texture, visible pores, natural under-eye detail, individual hair strands, and accurate facial proportions. The white shirt should retain detail without becoming overexposed. The black suit should reveal its fabric and tailoring instead of blending into a featureless silhouette.
The crystal tumbler should exhibit believable refraction, reflections, liquid movement, and small highlights along its rim. The marble countertop should show restrained reflections rather than an exaggerated mirror finish.
Use cinematic dynamic range, controlled highlights, natural contrast, and premium commercial color grading. Keep the background warm and inviting, but avoid oversaturated orange tones.

AUDIO DESIGN — FULL AMBIENT SOUND
Audio begins immediately and continues seamlessly throughout the four-second clip.
Background conversations: A continuous, low-volume layer of several overlapping adult voices. Conversations should sound naturally distant, with varied vocal tones and rhythms. Individual words should not be intelligible, and no background speaker should become the dominant voice.
Lounge music: Soft, sophisticated instrumental lounge music plays continuously in the background. Use mellow jazz-inspired piano chords, a gentle upright-bass line, and understated brushed percussion. The music is warm, relaxed, and slightly reflective, with no vocals and no dramatic musical climax. It should sit beneath the ambient conversations rather than sounding like a prominent soundtrack.
Glassware: Include occasional delicate clinks from patrons setting down glasses and the bartender handling glassware. At approximately 2.7 seconds, the protagonist's tumbler makes a distinct but quiet contact with the marble counter. A faint ice clink follows naturally.
Bartender activity: Subtle liquid pouring, light glass handling, and occasional soft shaker sounds in the background. These sounds should be intermittent rather than constant.
Room ambience: Gentle indoor crowd murmur, faint movement of barstools, subtle clothing movement, and a realistic sense of an occupied indoor space with mild natural reverberation.
Audio balance: The ambient crowd and lounge music should form a cohesive background layer. Glass clinks should be audible but delicate. Avoid abrupt changes in volume, exaggerated echoes, loud laughter, or artificial-sounding voices. Do not include narration, dialogue from the protagonist, or any prominent foreground conversation.

MOTION AND REALISM REQUIREMENTS
Prioritize natural human movement, accurate anatomy, consistent character identity, physically correct hand movements, realistic glass handling, and continuous background activity. The protagonist should not change facial appearance, hairstyle, body proportions, suit design, or tie between frames.
The camera movement must be smooth and physically plausible, with no handheld shaking, sudden zooming, rolling shutter artifacts, or unnatural perspective distortion.
The scene should feel like a candid moment captured inside a real, upscale cocktail bar. Every movement, sound, reflection, and lighting transition should support the illusion of live-action footage.
